omada.mrhaas.net

How to set it, step by step

1. Power the access point and connect it to the internet.

2. Connect to the access point – its own WiFi, or a cable

3. Log in. A new device usually uses admin for both.

4. Go to System, then Controller Settings. You may need to enable it.

5. In "Controller Inform URL/IP Address" andika omada.mrhaas.net kisha Save.

6. Then add the access point here, using its MAC address.

7. Unplug the access point for 10 seconds and plug it back.

8. It appears here within about two minutes.
